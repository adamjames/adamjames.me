I've never really felt the urge to post opinion. Feels a little self-aggrandising. Partly, it's a feeling of not having something worthwhile to say (which is slowly changing) but also not really having the courage to be disliked. In $currentyear especially, it seems *someone* disliking *something* is simply inevitable. While I've so far avoided internet wrath, I don't much like the idea of a first-hand experience.

That said, if being up front that I value something or that I stand for something brings more of that into my life, it seems for the best.
